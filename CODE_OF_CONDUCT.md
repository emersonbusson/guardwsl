# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participation in GuardWSL must be respectful, constructive, and safe. Examples
of expected behavior include giving actionable technical feedback, respecting
different experience levels, protecting private information, and accepting
maintainer decisions made to preserve user data.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private
information, impersonation, and repeated disruptive behavior are not accepted.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, and public spaces where someone represents GuardWSL.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban
participants when behavior threatens people or the project. For a private
report, use the repository's
[private reporting channel](https://github.com/emersonbusson/guardwsl/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `Conduct report`. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.

Platform-level abuse can also be reported directly to
[GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
