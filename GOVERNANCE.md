# Governance

GuardWSL currently uses a maintainer-led model. The repository owner is the
final decision maker for scope, releases, security response, and contributor
access.

Decisions are guided by four priorities, in order:

1. preserve user data under uncertainty;
2. keep host observation bounded and non-mutating;
3. keep the v1 architecture small and auditable;
4. improve cleanup coverage and ergonomics.

Material behavior changes are proposed through issues and reviewed pull
requests. Security fixes may be prepared privately until coordinated
disclosure. Governance may evolve as a stable contributor group emerges.
